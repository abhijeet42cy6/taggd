Step 1 — Download Data Template
The template has every sheet pre-named with the correct column headers. Just fill in your numbers.
📂 Sheets included in the template:
FY_CONFIG
List of fiscal years & month labels (add new FY rows here)
MONTHLY_DATA
All monthly arrays: RevA/B/F, CMA/B/F, Collections, Headcount, Joiners, PPC, Unbilled, BadDebt
TOTALS
Full-year summary totals per FY
BY_REGION
Revenue & CM actuals/budget by region per FY
BY_VERTICAL
Revenue by Lateral vs Leadership per FY
PROJECTS
Account-level Rev Actual/Budget/Forecast, CM, Region, Vertical per FY
HOW_TO_USE
Instructions, field definitions, example rows
